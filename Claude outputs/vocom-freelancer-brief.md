## 1. Who Vocom Is, in One Paragraph

Vocom supplies fiber optic cable in North America. It was founded in Taipei in 1993 and incorporated in the US in 1998. Vocom doesn't manufacture cable. It specifies, sources and coordinates build-to-order cable from tier 1 manufacturers, using Fujikura Japan preform glass, and delivers it faster than much of the market. **Fiber is the company, and fiber is what this program sells.** Vocom AI is a separate division (section 2). It doesn't belong in fiber emails.

**Company websites:** vocom.ai (main site and fiber product pages). Fiber pages: vocom.ai/fiber-optic-cables and vocom.ai/loose-tube-fiber-optic-cable. Contact and conversion: vocom.ai/contact-vocomai.

### What Vocom sells in the fiber push

| Category | Examples | Typical buyer |
|---|---|---|
| Outdoor loose tube (OSP) | GCYFTY and GYFS, 12F to 864F | Regional fiber builders, ISPs, contractors |
| Aerial and duct | ADSS, GYFDTS | Regional builders, utilities |
| FTTH drop | Flat and toneable drop, 1F to 24F, pre-connectorized drops, field connectors | ISPs, open-access builders |
| High-count and rollable ribbon | 288F to 1728F+ rollable ribbon, e.g. GYFDXBPH | Data center distributors and builders |
| Micro cable and microduct | Air-blown micro cable, HDPE microduct bundles, composite vaults | OSP contractors on congested routes |
| Indoor and data center | OFNP/OFNR indoor cable, MPO/MTP trunks, LC and MPO assemblies | Data center distributors and assemblers |

### Lead times (use these exact figures)

| Build | Lead time |
|---|---|
| Standard | 6 to 8 weeks |
| Expedited (air freight) | 2 to 3 weeks |
| High-count or custom | Up to 10 weeks |

Lead time is the main reason buyers reply. Distributors tell the Vocom team that other suppliers are quoting months for high-count, rollable ribbon and micro cable. Say "months" as a general market observation. Don't put a specific competitor figure in writing.

### Approved proof points

- Founded 1993 (Taipei), US-incorporated 1998. **Use the founding year, not a count of years.** The site's "27+ years" and older "30+" / "16 years" copy don't agree, so leave years out.
- 100+ regions connected, 25,000+ clients (as stated on vocom.ai).
- Fujikura Japan preform glass, tier 1 manufacturing partners.
- Build-to-order in the specs buyers can't get quickly: rollable ribbon, high-count, micro cable.

### Claims you must not make

- That Vocom manufactures, produces or has a factory. Use "supply", "source", "specify" or "deliver".
- "In stock". Vocom is build-to-order and has no US warehouse.
- Any price, per-foot rate, discount, MOQ or freight term. Pricing goes to sales.
- "600 miles to a regional ISP in 10 weeks", "competitors quote 50 to 60 weeks", "over 16 years". These were in old copy and aren't verified.
- Anything about country of origin, tariffs, or BABA/Buy America compliance. If a prospect asks, route them to sales.
- Customer names from this brief, in any email.

## 3. The Two Personas

### Persona A: The Regional Fiber Builder (**primary cold email target**)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who** | Regional ISPs, open-access fiber networks, telecom infrastructure contractors, rural telcos, municipal broadband |
| **Company size** | Roughly 20 to 500 staff. Small telecom and fiber firms produced the real positive replies |
| **Titles** | Procurement or purchasing manager, materials manager, OSP manager or engineer, construction manager, VP or Director of Operations, estimator, owner or GM at smaller firms |
| **Regions that bought** | Mountain West (ID, MT, CO), California, Arizona, the Midwest, western Canada (AB, BC, SK, MB) |
| **What they buy** | Outdoor loose tube (GCYFTY, GYFS, 24F to 864F), ADSS, FTTH flat and toneable drop, pre-connectorized drops |
| **Pain** | Build schedules slip when cable is quoted in months. Funded builds (grants, RDOF-style programs) run on deadlines |
| **Hook** | A delivery date they can plan around, the 6 to 8 week standard lead time, and a spec-matched quote |
| **CTA** | A reply with their spec or route (fiber count, type, footage, needed-by date) for a lead time and quote |

### Persona B: The Data Center Distributor or Assembler (**mostly relationship-led, not cold email**)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who** | Distributors and cable assemblers supplying data center and AI infrastructure builds |
| **Titles** | Product line or category manager (fiber/datacom), purchasing director, VP Sales, owner |
| **What they buy** | High-count rollable ribbon (288F to 1728F), indoor OFNP/OFNR cable, MPO/MTP and LC assemblies |
| **Pain** | Can't source high-count rollable ribbon and micro cable fast enough from existing mills |
| **Hook** | A fast, flexible second or third source for the specs their current suppliers can't deliver quickly. Don't pitch Vocom as a replacement |
| **CTA** | Send a spec to price and check the lead time |
| **Note** | All of Vocom's largest open deals are Persona B, and they come from relationships and trade shows. Cold email to this persona should be small and tightly targeted. **Never email the accounts in section 5.** |

### A third group to know: stocking OSP and broadband distributors

Distributors that stock cable for contractors (for example, electrical and broadband supply houses). They're a channel into Persona A. Pitch them as a second source for constrained specs, not a replacement for their main mills. Several are already being worked by the sales team from trade-show contacts (section 5), so check the suppression list before you add any.

## 6. Always-Suppress Rules

On top of the named accounts in section 5, always suppress:

1. **Competitors:** AFL, OFS, CommScope, Corning, Clearfield, Prysmian, Dura-Line, Siemon, Senko, and any fiber cable manufacturer.
2. **Education, healthcare and government** (schools, universities, hospitals, law schools), except municipal broadband programs, which are a fit.
3. **Hyperscalers and tier 1 carriers** (Verizon, AT&T, Oracle, Cisco, Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta). Don't launch the "ESG LEADS" draft. It's labelled wrong copy.
4. Bounced, unsubscribed, asked to be removed, replied not interested.
5. Dead-mailbox companies (for example, Crown Castle after its sale).
6. **A cap of 3 contacts per company.** Some companies received 100 to 200+ emails in past campaigns (Kiewit, Digital Realty, Equinix, Crown Castle).

## 7. What the Last Program Showed (Audit, 17 Sep 2026)

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Leads loaded | 21,560 |
| Emails sent | 11,073 |
| Replies | 122 (about 96 auto-replies, 26 human) |
| Human replies from schools, universities or hospitals | 15 of 26 |
| Marked "Interested" in Instantly | 5 |
| Real buying interest | 3 (all small US telecom and fiber firms) |
| Meetings booked | 0 |

**What worked:** the Fiber [CORE] USA campaign produced all 3 real positives (6,687 sent). Small telecom and fiber companies replied even from generic Operations Manager titles.

**What didn't:**

- [INST] campaigns: 81% of sends went to education or healthcare, with zero positives.
- International regional: zero positives.
- Manufacturing 2: 110 bounces.
- Replies fell from 41 (June, about 2,000 sent) to 3 (September, about 1,800 sent). One campaign is in Bounce Protect, so deliverability is a likely issue but not confirmed.
- 17+ lookalike sending domains, several AI-branded, sending fiber copy.
- The same CTA in every step, and one message sent to every audience.

**Priorities for you:**

1. Fix deliverability first: warm-up, domain health, and retiring AI-branded domains for fiber.
2. Rebuild lists around Persona A look-alikes in the regions above.
3. Write separate sequences for Persona A, stocking distributors, and a small Persona B test.
4. Change the CTA across steps (spec request, then lead-time check, then a soft break-up).
5. Send every positive reply to sales the same day.

## 8. Voice and CTA

- Professional, calm, technical. The buyers are engineers and procurement people. No hype.
- Don't use: leverage, game-changing, cutting-edge, revolutionary, unlock, synergy, best-in-class, seamless, world class.
- No em dashes. US spelling ("fiber").
- One soft ask per email, no links in the first email.
- The main conversion is a reply with a spec or a call with sales. The landing page is vocom.ai/contact-vocomai.
